6.What the app can and can't promise

The app uses your phone's camera, GPS, compass and motion sensors to guide you back to a spot and to help line up two photos. All of those are approximate, and the result depends on the phone, the scene, the light, and how patient you are. It's a creative tool, and you shouldn't rely on it for surveying, measurement, evidence, or anything else where a few feet or a few degrees matter.
