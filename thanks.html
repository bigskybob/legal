Meanwhile, start thinking about your first shot

The app takes you back to where a photo was taken so you can take it again and see what's changed. So the fun part starts before you ever open it: which picture would you go back for?

  • The kids on the front steps, a few years on.
  • Your house before the remodel, or the garden the year you planted it.
  • The trailhead, the campsite, the corner cafe from that trip you keep meaning to repeat.
  • An old family photo of a place that's still standing.
  • Progress on a project, or on yourself: same spot, same angle, months apart.

Dig it out now. When the app lands you'll already know where you're walking.

If you'd rather get in early

There's a small beta running before launch. If you want to try it on your own photos and tell me what breaks, reply to the confirmation email and say so, and I'll move you over to the beta list.

It all happens on your phone. It never hits a cloud, an edge server or a data lake, and it's never used to train an AI model. It's a tool, not a big tech company.
